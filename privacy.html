Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Overview

KOCloud Companion is an open-source, self-managed companion for KOCloud on KOReader. It runs in your web browser and connects to cloud services that you configure yourself.

Google Drive access

KOCloud Companion uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. This permission is intended to let the app work with files that it creates or that are otherwise made available to it.

Book uploads

EPUB and PDF files selected for upload are sent directly from your browser to your Google Drive account. KOCloud Companion does not operate an intermediate server that receives or stores your ebook files.

OAuth information

Your Google Web OAuth Client ID may be stored in your browser's local storage for convenience. A Web OAuth Client ID is not a secret.

Google access tokens are kept only in the memory of the current page session by KOCloud Companion. They are not intentionally persisted in local storage by the application.

Self-hosting

KOCloud Companion is designed to be self-hosted. The person deploying the application controls the hosting location, Google Cloud project, OAuth configuration, and connected storage.

Data collection

The KOCloud Companion source code does not include its own analytics, advertising, tracking service, or application backend for collecting ebook content or Google Drive data.

Third-party services

When you connect Google Drive, your use of Google services is also subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies. A hosting provider may separately process normal web-server information according to that provider's policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated as KOCloud Companion gains new features or integrations. The current version should be published with the deployed application.
